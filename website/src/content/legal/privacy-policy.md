# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026/11/10

## In short

- We collect what we need to run your account, cloud sync, sharing and subscriptions, and to keep the service secure.
- We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.
- Other people see your synced files only when you share them, apart from the limited cases in section 4.
- Stripe handles your payment card. We never receive your full card number.
- You can change, export or delete your information. Section 9 explains how.

## 1. Who we are

LFInteractive LLC ("we", "us") runs the Lodestone website at lodestonelauncher.com and the Lodestone account service. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

Contact us at contact@lodestonelauncher.com or at 28 Clinton Ave Winslow Maine Unit #6 04901 USA.

This policy covers the website, your Lodestone account (sign-in, friends, cloud sync, sharing and subscriptions), and the emails we send about your account and billing. It does not cover the files on your own computer. Section 7 explains what the Lodestone launcher sends and to whom.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account details:** your email address, a password (stored only as a one-way hash), and optionally a display name and a username.
- **Sign-in with Google, Microsoft or GitHub:** your account ID with that provider and the email address the provider verified. We don't receive your provider password, and we don't keep other profile details from the provider.
- **Friends:** the friend codes and invite links you create, and the friend requests you send or receive.
- **Minecraft accounts you link:** the profile ID and username of each Minecraft account, and when you linked it. We keep a temporary device code only while a link is in progress. We don't keep Microsoft, Xbox or Minecraft access tokens.
- **Instances and synced files:** instance names; the files you sync, with their paths, sizes, modification times and SHA-256 checksums; the contents of those files; version history (who pushed each version, when, and from which device); and sharing settings.
- **Billing:** the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers for your account, your plan, billing interval, subscription status, and trial and renewal dates. Stripe collects and holds your payment card and billing address.
- **Messages:** anything you send us by email.

### Information collected automatically

- **Devices:** the name you give a device, its operating system, the launcher version, and when it was registered and last used.
- **Sign-in sessions:** whether a session started on the website or in the launcher, and when it started and was last used.
- **Security records:** sign-in attempts, including failed ones, and account actions such as password changes. Each record includes the IP address and the time.
- **Rate-limit counters:** counts of recent requests, stored under a hash of the address or account rather than the address itself.
- **Web server logs:** the requests our web server receives, including the IP address, the page requested and the time.
- **Cookies and browser storage:** see section 6.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- provide the service: sign you in, keep you signed in, run friends, sync, version history and sharing, process your subscription, and send verification and password-reset emails;
- keep the service secure: prevent abuse, detect account takeovers, enforce rate limits and investigate incidents;
- meet legal and accounting duties, including keeping billing records;
- send service messages, such as security notices, billing notices and changes to these policies.

We don't use your synced files for advertising. We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If UK or EU data protection law applies, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (accounts, sync and subscriptions); our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and improving it; compliance with legal obligations; and your consent where we ask for it.

## 4. Who receives information

We share personal information only as follows:

- **Cloudflare** protects and delivers the website, and stores synced file contents in R2 storage.
- **Stripe** handles payments and subscriptions. See its [privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **Google, Microsoft and GitHub** handle the sign-in you choose, and only for the steps needed to sign you in. See the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), the [Microsoft privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) and the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
- **Linode** runs our servers and database.
- **Linode Hosted Email Provider** sends account and billing emails.
- **Pwned Passwords** checks new passwords against known breaches. We send only the first five characters of the password's SHA-1 hash, never the password or the full hash. See [Pwned Passwords](https://haveibeenpwned.com/Passwords).
- **People you choose to share with.** Friends can see your username and your friendship. People you share an instance with can see its files and version history, and editors can propose changes to it.
- **Legal and safety reasons.** We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when needed to protect someone's safety or to enforce our terms.
- **Business changes.** If we merge with another business or sell part of ours, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

## 5. Where information is stored

Our servers and database are hosted by Linode in Newark, NJ USA. Synced file contents are stored in Cloudflare R2. Our providers may process information outside your country. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## 6. Cookies and browser storage

- **Sign-in cookie** (`__Host-lodestone_refresh`): keeps you signed in. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Strict, and it lasts 30 days. It is strictly necessary.
- **Sign-in state cookie** (`__Host-lodestone_oauth_state`): checks that a sign-in with Google, Microsoft or GitHub started in your browser. It lasts 10 minutes. It is strictly necessary.
- **Temporary browser storage:** a device-link code or a friend invite is kept in your browser's session storage while you sign in. It is removed after use, or when you close the tab.
- **Google Fonts:** the website loads its fonts from Google. Loading a page sends your IP address and browser details to Google.
- **Cloudflare:** Cloudflare may set cookies that protect the site from abuse.

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

## 7. The Lodestone launcher and the services it uses

The Lodestone launcher doesn't send usage data or crash reports to us. Crash reports are written to the instance's crash-reports folder and stay on your computer unless you choose to share them.

When you use the launcher, it connects to services that receive your requests and IP address and handle them under their own policies:

- **Microsoft and Mojang**, for Minecraft sign-in, skins, and Minecraft and Java downloads;
- **Mod loader and content services**, including Fabric, Quilt, Forge, NeoForge, Modrinth and CurseForge, for downloading loaders, mods and other content;
- **GitHub**, for checking for launcher updates.

When you use account features, such as signing in, friends or sync, the launcher also connects to our servers, as described in this policy.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **Account details:** while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, except for the records listed below.
- **Synced files and version history:** until you delete the instance or the version. Stored file contents that no version refers to are removed in regular cleanup within 30 days.
- **Sign-in sessions:** 90 days after a session ends.
- **Security records:** 12 months.
- **Web server logs:** 30 days.
- **Billing records:** 7 years after your subscription ends, to meet tax and accounting rules. Stripe keeps payment records under its own policy.
- **Temporary records:** verification links expire after 24 hours, password-reset links after 1 hour, and friend invite links after 7 days. Sign-in state records are removed about a day after they expire. Device-link and Minecraft link records are removed when they expire or finish. Rate-limit counters are removed automatically.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate information;
- have your information deleted;
- receive a copy of it in a portable format;
- restrict or object to some uses;
- withdraw consent where we rely on it;
- complain to a data protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office. In the EU, it is the authority in your country.

California residents can ask us to disclose what we collect, correct it and delete it, and can opt out of sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal information as the law defines those terms. You may use an authorized agent to make a request, and we won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.

You can already do these things in your account:

- change your username;
- see your sessions and revoke them;
- see your devices and revoke them;
- unlink a Minecraft account;
- remove a friend or end a share.

For anything else, email contact@lodestonelauncher.com from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act. We respond within 30 days.

## 10. Security

We store passwords as slow one-way hashes. Session and reset tokens are stored only as hashes. The website is served over HTTPS. We rate-limit sign-in and other sensitive requests, and we limit access to our systems to the people who need it.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

## 11. Children

The website and the service aren't directed to children under 13, or to children under the higher minimum age in your country. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
